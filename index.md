# Chart Studio

Chart Studio is a personal, non-commercial music chart application used by a single household (two people). It collects public music chart history and the users' own streaming playlists, and lets those users browse, analyse and play the charts.

It is private: there is no sign-up, no public access and nothing is sold or advertised.
